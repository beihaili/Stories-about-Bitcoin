## English

This repository has three parts:

| | What it is | Who writes it |
|---|---|---|
| **The Chronicle** ([`正文/`](../正文/), [`en/`](../en/)) | A 39-chapter history of Bitcoin, 1976–2025 | beihaili, with corrections from the community |
| **The Garden** (this folder) | An anthology of short stories, published one season at a time | Anyone, in any language |
| **The Archive** ([`sources/`](../sources/)) | Sources and fact-check tables for every chapter and story | Everyone |

The Garden is for the stories a history book can't hold: the first time you ran a node, the friend who lost his keys, the transfer that got your family's savings out, a letter to Hal Finney. Its spirit is cypherpunk: privacy, self-sovereignty, open code, and "don't trust, verify".

### What you can submit

| Type | Example | Sources |
|---|---|---|
| **Memoir** | The day I first used bitcoin; how my family used it in Venezuela | Not needed for your own life; needed for public events |
| **Portrait** | A real person: Hal Finney, Len Sassaman, an unknown miner | Required. Quotes must be verbatim |
| **Historical fiction** | The night the 184-billion-bitcoin bug was found | Must be labelled fiction. Never invent quotes for real people and present them as fact |
| **Letter** | To Satoshi, to Hal, to yourself in 2009 | Not needed |
| **Poem, lyrics, script** | Any form | Not needed |
| **Oral history** | An interview with an early participant | Written consent from the interviewee; anonymity allowed |
| **Translation** | Any Garden piece into another language | Credit the original and the translator |

Suggested length: 600–4,000 words. Short and true beats long and complete.

### How to take part

1. **Plant a seed.** Don't want to write? [Open a story seed](https://github.com/beihaili/Stories-about-Bitcoin/issues/new?template=story-seed.yml): "Does anyone know the story of…?" Someone else may write it.
2. **Submit a story.** [Use the submission form](https://github.com/beihaili/Stories-about-Bitcoin/issues/new?template=story-submission.yml) and paste your text, or open a pull request that adds a folder based on [`_template/`](_template/). You don't need to know Git.
3. **Editing.** An editor will reply with questions, not rewrites. Your voice stays yours.
4. **Fact-check.** Portraits and historical pieces get a fact-check table before publication.
5. **Publication.** Selected pieces are translated and published in that season's anthology (website, PDF, EPUB).

### Ground rules

- **No shilling.** No price calls, no token promotion, no project ads.
- **Fiction is labelled.** The first line of a fiction piece says so.
- **Protect people.** No one's real name, address or wallet-identity link without their consent, unless they made it public themselves.
- **Pseudonyms welcome.** If writing under your name is dangerous where you live, don't. You may sign your work with a PGP or Nostr key instead, so readers can tell your pieces are by the same person.
- **Disclose AI use** in `meta.yml` (see below). Text generated by AI without substantial rewriting by the author is not accepted.

### AI disclosure labels

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `human` | Written by a person; AI only proofread or fact-checked |
| `human-led` | Written by a person; AI drafted some transitions or suggested structure |
| `ai-assisted` | AI drafted; a person rewrote it paragraph by paragraph |

Translations are labelled separately, e.g. `ai-draft, human-reviewed`.

## 中文

这个仓库由三部分组成：

| | 是什么 | 谁来写 |
|---|---|---|
| **主干**（[`正文/`](../正文/)、[`en/`](../en/)） | 39 章比特币编年史，1976–2025 | beihaili 主笔，社区纠错 |
| **花园**（本目录） | 社区短篇故事集，按季结集出版 | 任何人，任何语言 |
| **档案**（[`sources/`](../sources/)） | 每一章、每一篇的来源和核查表 | 所有人 |

花园收的是一部历史书装不下的故事：你第一次运行节点的那个晚上、那个丢了私钥的朋友、让家里的积蓄逃出来的那笔转账、一封写给哈尔·芬尼的信。它的精神内核是密码朋克：隐私、自主、开源，以及"不要相信，去验证"。

### 可以投什么

| 类型 | 例子 | 来源要求 |
|---|---|---|
| **亲历记** | 我第一次用比特币的那天；我们家在委内瑞拉怎么用它 | 个人经历不需要；涉及公共事件的部分需要 |
| **人物小传** | 写一个真实的人：芬尼、Len Sassaman、一个无名矿工 | 必须附来源，引语只用原话 |
| **历史短篇小说** | 发现 1840 亿个比特币漏洞的那个晚上 | 必须标明虚构；不能给真实的人编造原话并当作史实 |
| **一封信** | 写给中本聪、写给芬尼、写给 2009 年的自己 | 不需要 |
| **诗、歌词、剧本** | 不限形式 | 不需要 |
| **口述史** | 采访一位早期参与者 | 受访者书面同意，可匿名 |
| **翻译** | 把花园里任何一篇译成另一种语言 | 注明原文和译者 |

建议篇幅 1,000–6,000 字。短而真，胜过长而全。

### 怎么参与

1. **种下一颗种子。** 不想写？[提一颗故事种子](https://github.com/beihaili/Stories-about-Bitcoin/issues/new?template=story-seed.yml)："有人知道……的故事吗？"也许别人会把它写出来。
2. **投稿。** [用投稿表单](https://github.com/beihaili/Stories-about-Bitcoin/issues/new?template=story-submission.yml)直接贴文章，或者照着 [`_template/`](_template/) 新建一个文件夹提 PR。不会用 Git 也没关系。
3. **编辑。** 编辑会给你提问题，而不是替你改写。文章里的声音始终是你的。
4. **核查。** 人物小传和历史类作品发表前会做一张核查表。
5. **发表。** 入选作品会被翻译，收进当季合集（网站、PDF、EPUB）。

### 底线规则

- **不喊单。** 不给买卖建议，不推币，不打项目广告。
- **虚构要标明。** 虚构作品第一行注明"本篇为虚构"。
- **保护他人。** 未经同意，不公开任何人的真名、住址，或钱包与身份的对应关系，除非对方自己已经公开。
- **欢迎笔名。** 如果在你所在的地方署真名有风险，就别署。你可以用 PGP 或 Nostr 密钥给作品签名，读者就能确认几篇作品出自同一个人。
- **在 `meta.yml` 里披露 AI 的参与程度**（见上表）。全文由 AI 生成、作者没有实质改写的稿件不收。

### 授权

花园里的所有作品和整本书一样，采用 [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/)。投稿即表示你同意这一授权。打赏直接给作者：在 `meta.yml` 里写上你自己的闪电地址。
