## English

On January 10, 2009, Hal Finney typed two words. Everyone who has touched Bitcoin since has had a first time of their own: the first node, the first transaction, the first lost key, the first time it mattered.

This season, we want those stories. Yours, or someone else's.

### Prompts (pick one, or ignore them all)

1. The first time you heard the word "bitcoin". Where were you?
2. The first transaction you sent, or received. Who was on the other side?
3. The first time you ran a node, and what the fan sounded like.
4. The first time you lost coins: a forgotten wallet, a hack, an exchange that vanished.
5. The first time bitcoin was the only way: capital controls, inflation, a frozen account, a border.
6. The first time you explained it to your parents.
7. A letter to someone who was there first: Satoshi, Hal, Len, Laszlo.
8. A real first in Bitcoin's history, told from a side no one has told it from.
9. The first time you doubted it.
10. Your first block.

Prizes: *to be announced before the call opens.* All selected stories are published in the Season 01 anthology with full credit.

### What the editors look for

- **Something only you could write.** A detail, a memory, a feeling no search engine has.
- **Showing, not explaining.** Let the reader reach the meaning on their own.
- **Restraint.** Cut the sentence that tells us what it all means.
- **Accuracy.** Real events and real quotes must be checkable.

### Rules

- Any language. Previously unpublished work only (personal blog posts are fine).
- Up to two entries per person.
- The [Garden ground rules](../README.md#ground-rules) apply: no shilling, label fiction, protect people, disclose AI use.
- Pseudonyms and key-signed entries are welcome.
- All entries are published under CC BY-SA 4.0.

### While the call is open

- **Seed of the week**: every week we share the best new seeds on Twitter, Discord and Nostr.
- **Reading night** (December): authors read their drafts aloud, live. Reading aloud is the best edit there is.
- **Editor's hours**: share a draft in Discussions and get questions back, not rewrites.

## 中文

2009 年 1 月 10 日，哈尔·芬尼敲下了两个词。之后每一个碰过比特币的人，都有过属于自己的"第一次"：第一个节点、第一笔转账、第一次丢掉私钥、第一次它真的派上了用场。

这一季，我们想收集这些故事。你自己的，或者别人的。

### 写作提示（任选其一，也可以都不理）

1. 你第一次听到"比特币"这个词，是在哪里？
2. 你发出或收到的第一笔转账。另一头是谁？
3. 你第一次运行节点的那个晚上，风扇是什么声音。
4. 你第一次丢币：忘掉的钱包、被盗、跑路的交易所。
5. 比特币第一次成为唯一的办法：资本管制、通胀、被冻结的账户、一道国境线。
6. 你第一次给爸妈解释比特币。
7. 写一封信，给最早在场的人：中本聪、芬尼、Len、Laszlo。
8. 比特币历史上一个真实的"第一次"，换一个没人讲过的角度来讲。
9. 你第一次怀疑它。
10. 你的第一个区块。

### 编辑看重什么

- **只有你写得出来的东西。** 一个细节、一段记忆、一种搜索引擎里找不到的感受。
- **呈现，而不是解释。** 让读者自己得出意义。
- **克制。** 删掉那句告诉我们"这一切意味着什么"的话。
- **准确。** 真实事件和真实引语必须能查证。

### 规则

- 不限语言。只收未正式发表过的作品（个人博客发过的可以）。
- 每人最多两篇。
- 遵守[花园底线规则](../README.md#底线规则)：不喊单、虚构要标明、保护他人、披露 AI 参与程度。
- 欢迎笔名，欢迎用密钥签名。
- 所有作品采用 CC BY-SA 4.0 发布。

### 征文期间

- **每周种子**：每周在推特、Discord、Nostr 分享本周最好的几颗种子。
- **朗读夜**（12 月）：作者在线朗读自己的稿子。朗读是最好的修改。
- **编辑开放时间**：在 Discussions 里贴出草稿，收到的是问题，不是改写。
